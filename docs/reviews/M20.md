## Details I filled in (confirmed by Dan 2026-10-29: "confirm a-k"; SPEC v0.15)

- (a) **History replaces the Placed orders tab** (same place, same address). Its All / Open buttons became All / Open / Received / Cancelled.
- (b) The range starts on **This school year** until one is chosen. Only the range is remembered; supplier and status start at Any and All each time.
- (c) **The range applies to every status.** An open order placed before the range isn't listed under Open; choose a wider range to find it.
- (d) **Last 30 days** is today and the 29 days before it.
- (e) **Custom:** both dates are needed; dates entered the wrong way round are swapped.
- (f) **A discount is shown as a minus** on screen and written as a negative amount in both CSVs, so Parts + Discount + Tax + Shipping + Fees = Total across a row in a spreadsheet.
- (g) **Supplier summary:** suppliers A–Z, each named as on its newest order; "REV" and "rev" are one supplier.
- (h) **Other** holds typed lines and any item line without a category. The lines CSV says "Other" for them too.
- (i) **Exports go straight to the share sheet** (no preview dialog, unlike the order list's export).
- (j) **Season starts** can be any date, including one ahead (This season then lists nothing until it arrives). **School year starts** can't be February 29.
- (k) The printed page lists the orders as filtered (range, supplier, status); its summaries follow the range and supplier, as on screen.
