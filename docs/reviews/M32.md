# M32 review: smaller photos in count kits (SPEC v0.27)

**Reviewed:** branch `m32-small-kit-photos`, stacked on `m31-review-fixes` (PR #54; merge with a merge commit, HANDOFF §5). Checked against SPEC v0.27. App version 1.7.1. **Date:** 2026-11-17 CST

## Request
Dan, 2026-11-17: "with images included the help kits are very large (220+ mb). is there a way to still include thumbnails of the images but reduce the kit sizes?" Asked two questions: the photo size (**320 px**, over 480 px and the 160 px list thumbnail) and which items get photos (**all items**, as now, over only the helper's groups).

## Why kits were large
Every kit holds every item's photo (search and scan reach any item), at the size the tablet keeps them: about 1024 px (§8). But a helper phone shows the count photo at most 96 CSS px tall (`.happ .wphoto`), and a helper tablet up to 300. List rows use a 160 px thumbnail made on the helper.

## What was built
| # | What | Where |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Each kit photo made small: JPEG, 320 px on the long side, quality 0.8. The smaller of the small copy and the stored photo goes in; a photo the browser can't read goes in as before | `makeKitPhoto` in `src/io/image.ts`; `KIT_PHOTO_LONG_SIDE` in `src/domain/image-size.ts`; `src/data/kit-photos.ts` |
| 2 | Small copies kept for the session by item and photo save time, so Send all kits makes each one once | `src/data/kit-photos.ts` |
| 3 | `buildKit` reads the photos in its transaction and makes them small after it (canvas work would let the transaction close) | `src/data/helpers.ts` |
| 4 | The busy button says "Making photos small… n of m"; Include photos says "(n photos, made small for phones)"; the toast gives the kit size | `src/ui/screens/count/Helpers.tsx` |
| 5 | SPEC v0.27 (changes, §18.1, decision log); Getting Started step 4; version 1.7.1 | `docs/SPEC.md`, `docs/GETTING-STARTED-HELPERS.md`, `package.json` |

The kit format is unchanged (the photos are just smaller JPEGs), so helpers on older versions read these kits, and the tablet's photos and backups are untouched.

The first run stopped m2, m15 and m27 in first-time setup with three of four PIN digits entered: the PIN pad clears itself just after it appears (`PinPad`'s `clearKey` effect), and on the busy machine the check had typed its first digit before that. **What to expect:** kits about **a fifth to a sixth** of the tablet's photo size, so a 220 MB kit should be about 40 MB. (An earlier estimate from pixel counts said a tenth; small JPEGs carry more fixed overhead per photo than that assumed.)

## Details for Dan to confirm
- (a) **JPEG quality 0.8** at 320 px. Lower (0.7) would save perhaps another fifth with more visible blockiness; not tried.
- (b) **The first kit with a big catalog takes a while** on the tablet, with "Making photos small… n of m" on the button. About 2,000 photos took no test here; at this computer's rate it would be about 40 s, and the tablet is likely slower. Later kits in the same session are quick. The small copies are not stored, so after the app is closed the next kit makes them again.
- (c) **The small copies stay in memory until the app is closed:** about a fifth of the photos' size (about 40 MB for a 220 MB library).
- (d) **Photos helpers take of new items** still go back in the counts file at the usual 1024 px, so the tablet gets a full photo when it accepts the item.
