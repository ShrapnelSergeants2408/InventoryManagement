## Findings in this review (fixed)

1. **Labels printed in catalog order even when chosen by location.** Chosen "By location", they now print in walk order (then "No location"), so they come out in the order they go on the bins. The grouping moved to `domain/labels.ts` (`labelGroups`) with tests. An item whose category isn't in the list now gets its own group instead of dropping out of the print.
2. **Two quick nudge taps could count once** (both started from the same value). The latest value is now read from a ref. The browser check now taps three times in the same frame and expects +1.5 mm.
3. **"Try again" on the camera-blocked screen stayed on that screen**, because the camera state stayed "denied". Found by the browser check while it was being written; fixed before the first commit.
4. **Test gap: nothing checked the QR error-correction level.** The round-trip test now asserts level M, as decoded by zxing.
5. **Class-name clashes** (`.crow`, `.box`, `.steps` already existed for other screens) made the group rows look like Category cards and wrapped the step indicator. Renamed; found in the first screenshot.

## Decisions made while building (within the spec)

- **22805 margins:** the 4 × 6 grid of 1½ in squares is centred on the page (0.6875 in all round). The mockup used 0.625 in; Avery's published figure wasn't found. 5160 and 5163 use the published template figures. All are marked "verify against package" in the code (SPEC §15 Q2).
- **Plain paper:** cells are the QR plus 0.6 in wide and 0.55 in tall for the name, fitted inside 7.5 × 10 in and centred: 24, 12 or 6 per page for a 1, 1½ or 2 in QR.
- **QR codes** use error correction M and a 2-module quiet zone; the label's own white padding adds to it. A 5160 label's code is version 1 (21 modules), about 0.8 mm per module.
- **A code seen again within 2.5 s of being handled is ignored**, so "Done — scan next" doesn't reopen the label that's still in front of the camera.
- **The same barcode on two items** (possible by typing it in the editor) shows both, to pick from. Attaching from Scan refuses a code that's already on another item.
- **A team label for an item that isn't on this tablet** says so, instead of offering to attach a team label as a barcode.
- **The print pages are always rendered** (hidden on screen), so printing never depends on a render finishing in time.
- **The camera is released** when Scan closes and while the app is hidden.
