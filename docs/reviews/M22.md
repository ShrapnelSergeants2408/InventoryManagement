## Details I filled in (confirmed by Dan 2026-10-29: "accept a-h")

- (a) **Paid from is free text**, not a managed list like suppliers. Names used before are offered as you type; "school budget" and "School Budget" count as one source in the summary, named as on the newest order.
- (b) **One budget, for the school year** (the §21.5 "This school year" range), not per season or per funding source. Spent is every order's total (parts − discount + tax + shipping + fees) placed this school year, every supplier, cancelled ones left out. The range and supplier chosen above don't change it, so the card always answers "how much of this year's budget is left".
- (c) Both new settings are in More › Reminders & limits and need the PIN, like the other settings. The budget is blank (no budget) until set.
- (d) **Overdue** means placed or partly received, and placed more than the set number of days ago: with 14, day 15 is overdue. Received and cancelled orders never are.
- (e) **The shop status strip leaves out backup status**, because the backup reminder banner already sits right above it. Stale prices appear only when more than half the priced order lines are stale (the F14 banner rule), so the strip isn't permanently full. The order links open the History tab as it was last left; they don't switch it to Open.
- (f) **The Order screen's search (F12) is unchanged**; only the Inventory search (F4) gained category, subcategory and barcode. The order form's "+ Add line" search and helper search use the F4 search, so they gained them too.
- (g) **Recently viewed** records an item when its detail card opens, from anywhere (list, scan, search). It's kept on this device only, like the remembered filters; deleted items drop out. It shows only while the search box is focused and empty.
- (h) The **lines CSV** has no Paid from column: funding belongs to the order, and it's in Export CSV.
