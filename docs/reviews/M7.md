## Findings in this review (fixed)

1. **Remove wrapped to a second line on the detail card** (landscape, 3 buttons in the 370 px column). The mockup uses smaller photo buttons (`.pacts .btn`: 48 px high, 16 px text). Copied; all three now fit on one line, still 48 px.
2. **The older browser checks' touch-target scans would count the 1 px hidden file inputs.** The scans now skip `.vh` elements, as `m7-ui-check` does; the visible label buttons are still measured.
3. **Test gap: the thumbnail change notice had no check.** Its mutant survived, because the list check leaves the list and comes back, so the list reloads its thumbnails anyway. The place it matters is the **delete confirmation on the detail card**: its thumbnail stays mounted while the photo is replaced, and without the notice it showed a broken image (the old, revoked URL). A check now opens Delete after a replacement and expects the new thumbnail; the mutant is caught (`[0,0]` instead of `[80,160]`).

## Decisions made while building (within the spec)

- **Remove asks first on the detail card** ("Remove this photo?" with Keep photo / Remove photo). It needs no PIN, but there's no undo, and a starter photo only comes back from a backup. In the editor, Remove only marks the photo; Save or Cancel decides.
- **The camera button reads "Retake" once there is a photo** on the detail card (mockup 2a). In the editor it stays "Take photo" (mockup 3a).
- **Picked files:** anything that isn't `image/*` is refused before decoding, as is anything over 40 MB. A file with no type is tried (some gallery apps don't send one). A picture the tablet can't decode (e.g. HEIC, if Chrome can't read it) gets a message, and nothing changes.
- **Taken photos are marked on this tablet** (`taken` in the photo row) so the card can say "Team photo." instead of the vendor note.
- **A photo change counts as an inventory change** (`lastChangeAt`), since photos are in full backups.
