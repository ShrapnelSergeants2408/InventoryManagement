# H3: two parts, Find, Browse, scanning and labels

**Branch:** `h3-two-parts`, from `main` after PR #6 · **SPEC:** v3.0 §15 H3 · **App version:** 0.1.3 · **Date:** 2026-11-03

## What was built

The approved mockups (`docs/mockups/h3/`, Dan 2026-11-03) came first; this is the code behind them.

**Two parts (F21):**
- A **Household | Holiday** switch at the top of the main screens, the current part in its color: Household rust, Holiday evergreen.
- Each part has its own five-tab bar with **Scan** raised in the middle:
  - Household: Find · Browse · Scan · Lists · More;
  - Holiday: Season · Find · Scan · Lists · More.
- The phone remembers the part used last. A first launch opens Holiday (§12.5).
- Detail screens (item, bin, spot, editors) show their part in the top bar's color and name it. Opening a record of the other part switches the tab bar to that part.
- Scanning a label, or typing a bin code, from the other part opens that record and switches part. A toast says "Switched to Holiday · this label is a Holiday bin", and its **Undo** returns to the part and the Scan screen you were on.

**Data (schema v3, `src/data/db.ts`):**
- Areas, bins, items, categories and tags each carry a `part`. Spots follow their area. Seasons are Holiday only.
- Seasons gain **setup and takedown dates** ("MM-DD", wrapping over New Year), with the §3 defaults (Christmas Nov 25 – Jan 6).
- Items also store three Holiday fields, kept blank until H4/H6: usual display, display while out, and when last checked.
- Categories and tags moved to new tables keyed by part and name (`categoryList`, `tagList`), because "Other" is now in both parts and a primary key can't change in place.
- **The upgrade from 0.1.2** runs the first time 0.1.3 opens, in one step (§3):
  - every area, bin, item, category and tag goes to Household;
  - bins and items lose their season;
  - the seasons stay, now Holiday's, with their default dates;
  - Holiday gets its default categories and no areas.
- The "update needed" guard still works: a 0.1.2 copy that finds the v3 database writes nothing (tested).

**The rules (`src/domain/`):**
- `parts.ts`: one part's view; which part a record is in; moving an item or bin to the other part; and the 0.1.2 conversion (`fromV2`), which the upgrade and the restore both use.
- `integrity.ts`, `validate.ts` (§3, checked on restore and setup):
  - names unique within a part;
  - area letters and bin codes unique across both parts;
  - no link across parts;
  - no season on a household bin or item.
- `seasons.ts`: "Seasons on now" (§4). The lead is a setup starting today, else the nearest takedown. Then "Also now" and the next three under "Coming up".
- `find.ts` (F1): items, bins and spots of one part, with paths. Bins match their code ("g1" finds G-01), name, path and season.
- `browse.ts` (F2):
  - By place: Area › Spot › Bin, loose items under their spot, then bins with no spot, then "No home yet".
  - By season (Holiday): Season › its bins, then "Not in a bin", then "No season".
  - By category is the H2a view.
- `scan.ts` (F3): item, bin and spot labels and typed ids, typed bin codes, and product barcodes, all across both parts.
- `labels.ts` (F10, §9):
  - label faces for bins, spots and items on each layout;
  - the bin contents summary (§4);
  - "Label out of date" (§4);
  - choosing bins by area or season, and spots by area.

**Screens:**
- **Find:**
  - one search box over the current part, with results grouped Items / Bins / Spots (mockup 1);
  - "n matches in Holiday" opens the other part's Find with the same search;
  - with the box empty: the part's recently opened records, and in Holiday the three ways into Browse.
- **Browse:**
  - views remembered per part, with filters and Select › Move to… / Category…;
  - group headers for spots and bins have **Open**;
  - item rows lay out for a phone.
- **Season (Holiday home, mockup 2):**
  - the lead season's card, Also now and Coming up;
  - until H4, a season's button opens Browse by season for it.
- **Lists** (read-only until their milestones):
  - Household: Out on loan, Warranties ending;
  - Holiday: To buy (empty until H6), Needs attention, Out now.
- **More, per part:**
  - Areas & spots, Categories and Tags of that part, and in Holiday Seasons, now with dates;
  - Labels;
  - Backup & restore (both parts), Settings, About.
- **Bin screen:** Print / Reprint label, and a "Label out of date" note with Reprint.
- **Spot screen:** Print label.
- **Labels:**
  - choose Bins, Spots or Items;
  - bins grouped by season (Holiday) or area;
  - "n bin labels out of date · Select them".
  - A bin label on 5163 shows the code large, the name, the season as an outlined chip, and up to 6 contents lines, then "+ n more".
- **Move (item or bin):** the picker has **Show Household places** / **Show Holiday places**. Choosing one there turns the button into "Move to Household", with a line saying what will be cleared.
- **Set up:** Start fresh loads both parts' categories and the seasons with dates, then offers **Add Holiday storage areas**, **Add Household areas** or **Later**. Each part has its own area suggestions.

**Backup (§7):**
- The full backup holds both parts:
  - `part` columns in areas, bins, items, categories and tags;
  - the season dates;
  - the three new item columns;
  - per-part counts in the manifest.
- Restore reads a 0.1.2 ZIP the same way the phone upgrades.
- A 0.1.1 ZIP is refused with "this is a backup from version 0.1.1, which this version can no longer read" (§3). The H1 backup reader was removed.

## Details I decided (for Dan to confirm)

- **(a) The Season home before H4.** H4 brings the setup list and season runs. Until then:
  - the lead is a setup starting today, else the nearest takedown (on Nov 3 that's Halloween, takedown Nov 5);
  - each season's button is **Open <season>**, which shows its bins and items;
  - a line says the lists come in the next update.
- **(b) Holiday's Find tab** puts **By season / By place / By category** above Recently viewed. Holiday's Browse screen lights the Find tab.
- **(c) Browse has no search box.** Find does searching, as in the mockups. By place is the first view. By season leaves out seasons with nothing in them, so the ten defaults don't fill the screen.
- **(d) Scanning a bin or spot label opens its screen directly.** Mockup 5 shows the bin itself. An item label still opens the quantity panel. Undo after a bin or spot scan goes back to Scan in the part you were in.
- **(e) A bin always stores its part**, not only when it has no spot. It must still match its spot's.
- **(f) Item labels follow the §9 table:**
  - 5160: the name only (H2a also printed the path);
  - 5163: name and path (H2a also printed the category).
  - Bins always start on **5163**, whatever layout was used last.
- **(g) A label counts as printed when you tap "Open print dialog".** The app can't tell whether the printer finished. "Label out of date" compares what's in the bin now with what was on the label then (stored items only, as §4).
- **(h) Moving to the other part.** The confirmation is the picker's button ("Move to Household") and a line under it. Tags are cleared both ways, because each part has its own Tags list. A category with no match becomes "Other", which is added to that part's list if it's missing.
- **(i) A new area's letter** skips letters used by the other part (Holiday "Storage room" is S, so a Household "Shed" gets H). A typed letter that's taken names the other area and its part.
- **(j) The Lists tabs are read-only for now.** Returned, Fixed, Put back and ticking To buy come with H4–H8.
- **(k) Phone layout stopgaps** until H7's bottom action bar:
  - on detail screens, the action buttons sit two to a row under the photo;
  - Browse rows put the quantity stepper on its own line.
- **(l) Recently viewed** keeps the last 40 records, so each part can show its 8.

## Phone check (once merged and deployed)

1. **Before updating:** on the phone running 0.1.2, More › Backup & restore › **Full backup**, and save it to Files.
2. Open the app and wait for the Reload banner. More › App version should show **0.1.3**.
3. The app opens in **Holiday › Season**: today's lead season with its dates, Also now, Coming up.
4. Tap **Household** at the top:
   - the tab bar changes to Find · Browse · Scan · Lists · More, in rust;
   - your 0.1.2 items are all here, and **Browse › By place** shows your areas.
5. **Holiday › More › Areas & spots:**
   - add your storage areas (Storage room, Attic…), with a spot each;
   - add a bin there with the Christmas season, and an item in it.
6. **Household › Scan › Type a code:** type that Holiday bin's code (e.g. S-01).
   - It opens the bin in green with "Switched to Holiday".
   - **Undo** brings you back.
7. On the bin: **Print label** → 5163 preview with the big code, season and contents. Then use **Save to Files** or AirPrint.
8. **Household › Find:**
   - type something only Holiday has; the line "n matches in Holiday" opens it;
   - close the app and reopen it: it opens in the part you used last.
9. If anything looks wrong with the upgraded data, **More › Backup & restore › Restore** the backup from step 1. A 0.1.2 backup restores into 0.1.3 the same way.

## Independent review

A separate agent, which didn't write the code, reviewed the database upgrade, backup and restore, and the part rules in the data layer. It ran the tests plus its own probes outside the repository.

**Result:** nothing serious. It confirmed:
- the v2 → v3 upgrade handles every table and can't stop half-way;
- a 0.1.2 copy can't reopen or write to the upgraded database;
- the backup round trip works for both parts;
- every write keeps homes, categories and tags in their own part, and every transaction lists the tables it touches.

Its two minor findings were fixed in this PR, with a test:
1. A blank `part` cell in a 0.1.3 backup was read as Household. It's now refused ("part is required"). Only a 0.1.2 file, which has no `part` column at all, reads as Household.
2. A ZIP whose files disagree about having a `part` column is now refused. This covers `items.csv` re-saved without the column beside 0.1.3 `areas.csv` and `bins.csv`. Before, the whole Holiday part would have moved to Household.

It also pointed out that the upgrade clears the season on 0.1.2 bins and items, as §3 says. That can't be undone, which is why step 1 of the phone check is a Full backup.

Tests are now 464 (one added).
