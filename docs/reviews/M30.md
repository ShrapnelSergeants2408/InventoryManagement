## Details for Dan to confirm
Details (a)–(g) were confirmed in the interview. Two more were decided while building:
- (h) **The PIN is asked at Add items and at Undo,** after the preview, not before the file picker (SPEC §23.6). It matches the price import, and a PIN pad between the tap and the picker could lose the browser's permission to open the picker.
- (i) **A barcode another item already has is left off** the new item and counted in the preview, so a scan is never ambiguous (F21).

Also worth knowing: photos are stored as a restore stores them (not resized), and an item whose barcode, name or part number matches is skipped, never merged.

- (j) **The AndyMark category table** (`CATEGORY_MAP` in `scripts/andymark-lib.mjs`) is a first guess at about 45 product types; only Bushings, Nuts and Screws were seen in AndyMark's feed. After a full run, send me the types the script lists as unplaced.
