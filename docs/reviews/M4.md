## Findings from reading the code

1. **Changed from M1 (deliberate):** order lines were sorted A–Z by name. They now follow catalog order (by id: starter items, then team items), matching mockup 4a and the Inventory list. Name order would put "10-32 x 1 in." before "10-32 x 3/8 in.". Supplier groups are still A–Z with `other` last.
2. **Fixed (robustness):** groups were keyed by the exact supplier spelling, so imported data with "rev" and "REV" would have made two REV groups with two subtotals. Groups now ignore capitals (test and mutant).
3. **Added:** the order CSV is for Excel or Google Sheets and is never read back by the app. Text a spreadsheet would run as a formula (=, +, @, or "-" before a letter) gets a leading apostrophe. A part number like "-20" or "10-32" is untouched. This isn't spelled out in D4; tell me if you'd rather have raw text.
4. **Fixed (from the screenshots):** banner icons and buttons wrapped onto their own lines, and the export preview was cut off on the right. The preview now shows Supplier, Part #, Item, Order qty and Line total, and says the file has all 13 columns.
5. **Accepted, noted:** "Continue count" opens the Count placeholder until M9. The price-refresh steps name the backup and price import, which arrive in M5, and say so.
6. **Accepted, noted:** Export starts the share sheet synchronously from the button tap (`canShare`, then `share`, before any await), which Android needs. Whether the CSV goes to the share sheet or to Downloads on the Tab S8 is a Stage 5 check (Q1).
7. **Mockup vs data:** mockup 4a shows 125 lines, 8 suppliers and "1 without price" (mockup data). The starter catalog gives 124 lines, 7 suppliers and all prices, which matches SPEC §12 and the M1 test.
