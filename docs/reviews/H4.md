# H4: the Holiday setup

**Branch:** `h4-holiday-setup`, from `main` after PR #8 · **SPEC:** v3.0 §15 H4 · **App version:** 0.1.4 · **Date:** 2026-11-03

## What was built

H4 makes the Holiday part usable for Christmas setup on one phone (SPEC §15): displays (F22), Start setup and the setup list with season runs and tasks (F25), Unpack (F5), setup photos (F8), and the season calendar with an `.ics` file (F23). The setup list follows the approved H3 mockup (`docs/mockups/h3/Setup-List.dc.html`).

**Data (schema v5, `src/data/db.ts`):**
- Three new tables, empty after the upgrade: `displays`, `seasonTasks` and `seasonRuns` (§3). Nothing already stored changes.
- A photo may carry the season it shows (`seasonId`), for a display's setup photos. Photos from before 0.1.4 have none.
- The items' usual display and display-while-out fields (added empty in H3) are now used.
- The "update needed" guard still holds: an older copy that finds the v5 database writes nothing. The tests open it with an H1 copy and a 0.1.2 copy, and check the general newer-schema case; a 0.1.3 copy goes through the same check.

**Rules (`src/domain/`):**
- `holiday.ts`:
  - which year's run a season is on (§3: the year its setup starts), so Start setup on Nov 20 and a look on Jan 2 both mean this Christmas;
  - the setup list: the season's bins in walk order, its items by usual display in walk order then "No display", its setup tasks, and progress for each and in all.
- `seasons.ts`: "Seasons on now" (§4) now counts a run that's underway, so a setup started early, or a season still up after its takedown date, shows on the home.
- `ics.ts`: the calendar file (RFC 5545).
- `validate.ts`, `integrity.ts`: the new records are checked on restore, with their links (an item's displays, a task's season, the tasks a run ticked, a photo's season).
- `find.ts`: in Holiday, an item also matches its usual display and where it's out.

**Screens:**
- **More › Displays** (Holiday only): add, edit name and notes, reorder (the setup walk-through), remove. Removing one in use says what uses it.
- **A display's screen:** its setup photos, one season at a time (season chips), what's on it now, and what usually goes there with where each is stored.
- **A season's screen** (from the Season home, or Coming up):
  - where this year's run is ("Not started · setup from Nov 25", "Setting up · 12 of 30 done", "Up · take down by Jan 6"), with **Start setup** or **Setup list**;
  - its setup tasks and takedown tasks: add, edit, reorder, remove;
  - its setup notes and photos;
  - its displays, each with how many of the season's items go there;
  - Bins and items (Browse by season), **Add to Calendar**, Dates and notes (More › Seasons).
- **The setup list (F25), as in the mockup:**
  - a green header with the progress bar: "17 of 47 done · started today, 9:12 AM";
  - **1 · Bins to bring out:** tick or tap **Out**; bins already out fold into "+ 3 more done (…)";
  - **2 · Put out:** the season's items under their usual display, each display with its count and a **Photos** link; a tick puts the item out on its usual display, an untick puts it back; **Unpack a whole bin**;
  - **3 · Tasks:** ticked for this year; new tasks can be typed straight in;
  - at the bottom, **Scan a bin** and **Finish setup**.
- **Unpack (F5)**, on a Holiday bin's screen and from the setup list:
  - every stored item ticked, each going on its usual display;
  - "Put them on" changes the place for the batch: a display, or somewhere typed;
  - each item can have its own display;
  - "Set STORAGE-01 out too", ticked;
  - links to the setup photos of the displays they're going to.
- **Holiday item screen:** its usual display (a link to the display), and **Put out…** or **Put back**.
- **Item editor (Holiday):** "Usual display".
- **Season home:** each season's button is its next step: **Start setup**, **Setup list**, or **Open** once it's up. Rows open the season's screen. **Add the season dates to Calendar** at the bottom.

**Backup (§7):** the full backup adds `displays.csv`, `season_tasks.csv`, `season_runs.csv`, and `season_id` in `files.csv`. A 0.1.3 backup, without them, restores with no displays, tasks or runs.

## Details I decided (for Dan to confirm)

- **(a) Start setup any time.** It's on the season's screen and the home, so Christmas can start before Nov 25. The run belongs to the year its window starts. While a run is underway the season shows on the home, even outside its dates.
- **(b) What leads the Season home.** A setup or takedown in progress, or a setup starting today, comes first; then the nearest takedown date. A season that's up waits for its takedown date. One still up after that date shows "Up · take down was due Jan 6" and leads.
- **(c) A tick before Start setup starts the run.** Opening a setup list directly and ticking something counts as starting.
- **(d) Unticking undoes.** An item goes back to stored in its home, a bin back to stored, a task unticked.
- **(e) Items are grouped by where they usually go**, not where they are now. Ticked items stay in place, struck through, showing where they went if that's somewhere else. Bins that are out fold away under "+ n more done".
- **(f) Unpack** starts with everything ticked, each item on its usual display. An item with no usual display goes out with no place (its chip reads OUT). The batch choice lists displays, then "Somewhere else (type it)…"; per item, only displays.
- **(g) Scan a bin on the setup list.** A bin of this season is set out, then Unpack opens if anything in it is still stored. An item label of this season puts the item out on its usual display. Anything else says so, with **Open it** for a bin of another season.
- **(h) Finish setup** names what's still unticked, and finishing returns to the Season home. **Reopen setup** on the list undoes a Finish tapped by mistake.
- **(i) Removing a display** clears it from items that usually go there. Items out on it stay out, keeping its name as typed text. Its setup photos are deleted. Renaming a display renames where its items are out.
- **(j) Removing a season** now also removes its tasks and runs, and untags display photos of that season.
- **(k) Setup photos.** On a display, one season at a time. The display opens on the season leading the home, else the first season. A season's photos can't be reordered on their own (the order is the display's whole carousel). A season's own photos (on its screen) are its general photos.
- **(l) Put out… / Put back on a Holiday item's screen.** Not in F16's list; it's the one-item way to do what the setup list does.
- **(m) The calendar file:**
  - "Christmas: set up" and "Christmas: take down" as yearly all-day events, with a 9 AM alert on the day;
  - Feb 29 becomes Feb 28, so it happens every year;
  - it goes through the share sheet, like a backup, else downloads.
- **(n) Find in Holiday** also matches an item's usual display and where it's out ("porch" finds the wreath).

## Phone check (once merged and deployed)

1. **Before updating:** More › Backup & restore › **Full backup**, and save it to Files.
2. Open the app and wait for the Reload banner. More › App version should show **0.1.4**.
3. **Holiday › More › Displays:** add the places you decorate (Mantel, Front porch, Dining table…), in the order you walk them.
4. Open a few Christmas items, **Edit**, and set their **Usual display**.
5. **Holiday › Season:** open **Christmas** (under Coming up, or Also now). Add a setup task or two. Tap **Start setup**.
6. On the setup list:
   - tick a bin, tick an item (check it shows OUT · Mantel on its screen), tick a task;
   - **Unpack a whole bin**, change one item's display, and take them out;
   - **Scan a bin** with the camera on a printed bin label;
   - **Finish setup**. The home shows Christmas "Up · take down by Jan 6".
7. **A display:** take a setup photo under the Christmas chip.
8. **Add to Calendar** (bottom of the Season home). Please tell me what the share sheet offers for `holiday-seasons.ics`, and whether Calendar adds the dates. This is the one part I couldn't test without an iPhone. If Calendar isn't offered, **Save to Files** and then tapping the file in Files should add them.
9. If anything goes wrong with the data, **More › Backup & restore › Restore** the backup from step 1. A 0.1.3 backup restores into 0.1.4.

## Independent review

A separate agent, which didn't write the code, reviewed the data side: the v5 upgrade and the "update needed" guard, every new write's transaction, links left behind by removals, the Holiday-only rules, and the backup round trip. It ran the tests and its own throwaway probes, and left nothing in the repository.

**Result:** nothing that corrupts the database or breaks a restore. Its findings, and what was done:
1. **Putting out a lent item erased who had it.** Fixed: a lent item can't be put out ("mark it returned first"), and lent items stay off the setup list until returned. Tested.
2. **A season left "up" (no takedown list until H6) would have blocked next year's Start setup.** Fixed: an unfinished run goes stale when the next year's window starts. Until then the season shows as overdue on the home; after that, the new year gets its own run. Tested.
3. **The bin-out writes didn't refuse a Household bin** (the screens never offered it). Fixed and tested.
4. **Restoring a 0.1.3 backup on a 0.1.4 phone empties the displays and season tasks without saying so.** That is what a restore does (it replaces everything), but the restore screen now warns: "This backup was made before displays and season tasks (0.1.4): the phone’s 2 displays and 3 season tasks will be removed." Tested.
5. **Notes, not changed:** removing a season reads every photo to untag it, which is slower with many photos but correct; and the whole-data check doesn't require a display-while-out only on items that are out, which is harmless today.
