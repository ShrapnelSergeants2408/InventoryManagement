## Details for Dan to confirm
Details (a)–(g) were confirmed in the interview; (h), (i) and (j) were confirmed by Dan on 2026-11-16. They were decided while building:
- (h) **The PIN is asked at Add items and at Undo,** after the preview, not before the file picker (SPEC §23.6). It matches the price import, and a PIN pad between the tap and the picker could lose the browser's permission to open the picker.
- (i) **A barcode another item already has is left off** the new item and counted in the preview, so a scan is never ambiguous (F21).

Also worth knowing: photos are stored as a restore stores them (not resized), and an item whose barcode, name or part number matches is skipped, never merged.

- (j) **The AndyMark category table** (`CATEGORY_MAP` in `scripts/andymark-lib.mjs`) is a first guess at about 45 product types, confirmed as the starting table. A type it doesn't know goes to Misc with the vendor's type as the subcategory. After a full run, send me the types the script lists as unplaced.

## The AndyMark pilot (2026-11-16)
Dan asked for a pilot with images, vendor categories where possible, and a way to avoid the 429.
- **429:** the script already waited 2 s between requests, and AndyMark still refused the first live attempt. It now waits out a 429/503 (as long as `Retry-After` says, up to 5 minutes), slows down, and retries up to 3 times; the default delay is 3 s. In the pilot it was refused twice at 3 s and then went through.
- **Photos (`--images`, opt-in):** 12 products gave 41 parts and 12 photos (0.8 MB, 1024 px wide); the ZIP was read by Add items from a file with 0 skipped rows and 0 missing photos. The pilot ZIP was written outside the repository and not kept. A run can't show the photos are *allowed*: AndyMark's terms permit downloading for one's own non-commercial use but limit reproduction and display, `robots.txt` (site and image host) doesn't forbid it, and only AndyMark can authorize the team's use. Ask them in writing before relying on the photos, and keep them out of the public repository.
- **Categories:** AndyMark has no category hierarchy in its feed (215 flat collections, one holding 2,155 discontinued products), only a product type per product. Known types map to the team's categories; others go to Misc with the type as the subcategory.
- **A catch:** one product (Electronic Bundles Builder) has a variant for every option combination, so 12 products became 41 parts. A full run will have many of these; the importer lists them like any other row, but a mentor may prefer to leave them out.
- **Other vendors** (REV, Thrifty Bot, CTRE, Vulcan Spring, WCP, Bimba) are listed in SPEC §23.7 as future, each after its own terms check.
